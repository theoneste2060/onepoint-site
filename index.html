Common questions

Quick answers about what we do and how we work. If yours isn't here, call us.

What services does OnePoint Logistics provide?

We coordinate venue sourcing, accommodation, airport transfers, transportation, catering, conference equipment, printing, event branding, interpretation, security, photography, entertainment and event staff, all through one local point of contact.

Do you provide event and conference logistics in Kigali?

Yes. We coordinate conference and event logistics in Kigali and across Rwanda for companies, NGOs, international organizations and institutions.

Can you arrange airport transfers and transportation?

Yes. We coordinate airport pickups and drop-offs at Kigali International Airport, and buses, vehicles and drivers for conferences, workshops, corporate events and field activities.

Can you arrange accommodation for conference participants?

Yes. We coordinate hotels and group accommodation for participants, teams, international visitors and event delegates.

Who do you work with?

Companies, NGOs, international organizations, institutions and other organizations that need reliable local logistics and event support in Rwanda.

Do you work outside Kigali?

Yes. We provide services across Rwanda according to your requirements and the nature of the assignment.

Why use OnePoint instead of managing several suppliers?

Instead of separate suppliers for venues, transport, catering, equipment and branding, you coordinate everything through one local partner.

Where are you based, and can I visit?

We are in Gahanga Sector, Kicukiro District, Kigali, near Gahanga Cricket Stadium. Customers are welcome to visit Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
